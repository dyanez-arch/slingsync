SlingSync 

      Free multicam sync by sound, for Premiere Pro. Lines up any number of cameras and
      recorders by their sound, no timecode needed, and can compact a day into one block.

    
Also available as a free desktop app for Mac (no Premiere needed, also exports
      audio for podcasts and audio editors).

    
Made by Daniel Yanez, documentary editor.
      Free and open source (MIT).
