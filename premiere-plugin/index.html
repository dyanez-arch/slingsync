SlingSync

Multicam sync by sound
Sync a Shoot Folder: pick the shoot folder (one subfolder per camera or recorder), check what was found, then Sync. Sync Selected Bin: the same for clips already in this project: select the shoot's bin (one sub-bin per camera or recorder) first. Sync Timeline: syncs the clips in the open sequence in a copy; if clips are selected, only those. If cameras or recorders share a track, it asks whether to put each on its own tracks. Compact Timeline: makes a copy of the open sequence with every gap where nothing was recording cut out, for review or a transcript.

SlingSync 

      Free multicam sync by sound, for Premiere Pro. Lines up any number of cameras and
      recorders by their sound, no timecode needed, and can compact a day into one block.

    
Also available as a free desktop app for Mac (no Premiere needed, also exports
      audio for podcasts and audio editors).

    
Made by Daniel Yanez, documentary editor.
      Free and open source (MIT).
